GameNight privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Versão em português

GameNight is an app for playing games with other people in the same place — either passing one phone from hand to hand, or with everyone on their own phone. This page explains, in plain words, what the app keeps about you, where that information lives and what you can do about it. The app itself is in Portuguese, so screen names are given in Portuguese with their meaning in parentheses.

Who is responsible

GameNight is made by Studio Zenbu, in Brazil, which decides how the app's data is handled. For anything about your data, write to suporte@studiozenbu.com.

The name you type

In Ajustes (Settings) you can type a name (up to 24 characters) for the other players to see. That name is kept on your phone. It is sent to Firebase (the Google service that hosts the rooms) only when you create or join a room played on more than one phone, so the other players know who is who. Apart from that and the usage statistics and card ratings described below, we collect no other data about you: no email, no phone number, no precise location.

There is no account

The app uses Firebase's anonymous sign-in so that rooms and card ratings work. This creates an anonymous identifier for your device without asking for an email, password, sign-up or any login. GameNight has no user accounts.

What stays only on your phone

Besides your name, these are kept only in your phone's local storage: your recent games, the cards this phone has already shown (so they don't repeat), the tables you set up in the Toolbox (which tools, their settings and any name you give them), your theme choice (light, dark and so on) and your answer about usage statistics. This information never leaves the device, not even in the phone's automatic backup.

What a room holds during a game

While a game is in progress, Firebase holds: the room code (four letters or digits), who is in the room (each person's name and anonymous identifier), what players write during the game (words, depending on the game) and the state of the game (scores, teams, votes, assigned roles and card order). This is the minimum needed for the game to work in real time across the phones.

When a room is deleted

When the person who created the room leaves it, the whole room is deleted from Firebase at once, with the names of everyone who was in it. If that person closes the app without leaving the room, or their phone switches off or loses its connection, the room waits for them to come back; if they stay away more than half an hour, it is deleted when they open the app again. A room nobody came back to is deleted by us within 30 days at most.

Using "Apagar meus dados" (Delete my data, see below) always clears everything the app saved on your phone. If an anonymous session is active, it is ended and the app asks Firebase to delete that identifier; without a connection, it is only ended on this phone. With a connection, it also deletes the room you created and takes your name out of someone else's room; a name still left in an open room is deleted when that room closes.

Usage statistics

To learn which games people play and what to improve, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records when a game starts, each round played, when the game ends and when the group quits partway through — with the name of the game and whether it was played on one phone or several. The service also records on its own when the app is opened, how long it is used and which screens are shown, and collects technical information such as the device model, system version, country or approximate region, and an identifier for the app on that phone.

These statistics include neither your name nor anything typed during games, and they are not used for advertising: the app does not access the phone's advertising ID. "Apagar meus dados" changes the app's identifier, so what was played before is no longer linked to this phone.

The app counts these statistics from the first time it is opened, and says so that first time, with the option to switch them off. You can switch them off or back on at any time in Ajustes → Privacidade → Estatísticas de uso (Settings → Privacy → Usage statistics); once off, nothing more is sent.

Crash reports

When the app closes by itself or a screen fails, a technical report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google) so we can fix the problem: the device model, the Android version, the app version and the error that happened. The report carries no name, no room code and nothing typed in the games, and it does not depend on the Usage statistics switch.

Card ratings

At the end of a game the group can choose to mark cards as "Boa" (good) or "Difícil demais" (too hard). These marks are sent to Firebase with only the name of the game, the cards marked, the mark and the time — without your name and without a device identifier — and are used only to improve the decks. Nobody outside the studio sees them, and they are not linked to you.

Why we use this data

  • Rooms and the names in them: to make the game you opened work.
  • Usage statistics: our legitimate interest in understanding which games are played and what to improve, with data that does not identify you and that you can switch off in Ajustes (Settings) at any time.
  • Card ratings: our legitimate interest in improving the decks, with no data that identifies you.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in fixing the app's errors, with no data that identifies you.

Who receives the data and where it is kept

GameNight shows no ads and does not sell or share your data. The only third-party service the app uses is Google Firebase (Google LLC), which hosts the rooms, the usage statistics, the crash reports and the card ratings on our behalf. Google's servers may be outside Brazil, including in the United States; Google handles this data under its data protection terms (firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms) and explains what it does with it at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Security

Data travels encrypted between the phone and Firebase, and the database rules only let someone into a room if they have its code. If a security incident could put players at risk or cause them harm, we tell Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD) and the people affected, as the LGPD requires.

How long it is kept

  • Rooms: until the person who created the room leaves it; if they closed the app without leaving, until they open the app again after half an hour; and an abandoned room, 30 days at most. "Apagar meus dados" (Delete my data) also deletes the room you created.
  • Usage statistics: 2 months; after that Google deletes the details and only totals remain, which link nothing to any phone.
  • Crash reports: 90 days; then Google deletes them.
  • Card ratings: while they are useful for reviewing the decks; they identify nobody.
  • Anonymous identifier: kept in Firebase until you use "Apagar meus dados" (Delete my data). Uninstalling the app does not delete it, but it is no longer used and carries none of your data.
  • What stays on the phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under Brazil's data protection law (LGPD), and the GDPR where it applies, you can ask to know what data we hold, correct it, delete it, take it to another service, and object to the statistics by switching them off at any time. Most of this is already in the app: Ajustes → Apagar meus dados (Delete my data) and Ajustes → Privacidade → Estatísticas de uso (Usage statistics). For anything else, write to suporte@studiozenbu.com. You can also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD) or to the authority where you live.

Children

GameNight is made for groups of teenagers and adults and is not aimed at children. The app does not ask for anyone's age and does not collect full names, email addresses, phone numbers or precise location: only the nickname you type and the anonymous identifier described above.

How to delete everything

In Ajustes → Apagar meus dados (Settings → Delete my data), the app always removes the information saved on your phone (name, recent games, cards already shown, Toolbox tables, theme and your answer about statistics) and changes the usage statistics identifier; the next time the app opens, it tells you about them again. The app icon you chose stays as it is. If an anonymous session is active, it is ended and the app asks Firebase to delete that identifier.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If the app starts collecting something new, it tells you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to suporte@studiozenbu.com.
